# Contributors

Ossuary is built by:

- **Ossuary Dev** — maintainer (project identity per the build process).

Contributors are added here when their first pull request is merged, unless they
ask not to be.

## AI assistance

Significant portions of the initial implementation were drafted with AI coding
assistants (see `ATTRIBUTION.md`, "AI tooling used during the build"), then
reviewed against the project's clean-room and quality rules before commit. No AI
output is committed without that review.
