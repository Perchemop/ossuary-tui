# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with other people, especially newcomers.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information
without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainer via a private channel (see `SECURITY.md` for the
private reporting mechanism). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated,
and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the common open-source Contributor
Covenant, in its own words.
